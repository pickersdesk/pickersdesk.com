New restaurants pick their equipment, suppliers and service companies weeks before they open. A new filing usually shows up one to three months ahead, while those decisions are still open. The Turnover tells you who they are.

  • Equipment dealers: they buy the kitchen before they open the doors.
  • Liquidators and used equipment: hear about closed kitchens before the auction listing goes up.
  • Service companies (refrigeration, hoods, install): new owners often bring in new vendors. Be the one they meet first.
  • Distributors: win the account before the first delivery.

How it works · The Turnover

From raw filing to your inbox.

  1. The state files it

    Every business day, California posts new liquor-license applications, transfers, issued licenses and surrenders.

  2. We pick it clean

    We keep the nine Bay Area counties, sort each filing into opening, changing hands or closing, and put it in plain English.

  3. You get the short list

    One email a week: who, where, and what stage they're at. Facts from the record, no opinions.
