Bay Area restaurants and bars opening, closing and changing hands.

Pulled straight from California liquor-license filings across all nine Bay Area counties. A new filing usually shows up one to three months before a place opens, while the owner is still choosing equipment, suppliers and service companies.

  • Equipment dealers reach new restaurants before they buy.
  • Liquidators and used-equipment buyers hear about closings first.
  • Service companies (refrigeration, hoods, install) meet new owners early.
  • Distributors pitch while the supplier list is still open.

How it works

From raw filing to your inbox.

  1. The state files it

    Every business day, California posts new liquor-license applications, transfers, issued licenses and surrenders.

  2. We pick it clean

    We keep the nine Bay Area counties, sort each filing into opening, changing hands or closing, and put it in plain English.

  3. You get the short list

    One email a week: who, where, and what stage they're at. Facts from the record, no opinions.
