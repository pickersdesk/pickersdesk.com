How we score

Each metro gets 1 to 5 points on six measures, out of 45: data access (counted twice: is there an open, machine-readable dataset we're allowed to use), data quality (counted twice: fresh, with job value, contractor and parcel number), whether one county portal covers most of the metro, permit volume, room versus existing lead services, and our own head start there.

Checked October 17, 2026 against the agencies' portals. Where a portal couldn't be reached we say so in the market's note; a few permit-volume scores (Indianapolis, Jacksonville, Oklahoma City, Richmond) are estimates from metro size because no count could be confirmed. A metro whose portal terms don't allow automated use or commercial reuse stays at the bottom until the agency gives permission. Want your area moved up?
