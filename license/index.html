    How to read the result

    • Status and dates

      CLEAR is CSLB's word for a license with no current disciplinary or suspension marks on the public record. The expiration date is the renewal date, not a verdict on the work.

    • Bond and workers' comp

      "Bond on file" means CSLB's list shows a contractor bond with no cancellation date. The workers' comp line is CSLB's coverage type: an insurance policy, a certificate of self-insurance, or an exemption (no employees declared).
