Auction alerts

Hear when a county posts its next sale.

One short email when a Bay Area county announces a new property sale or a notable surplus auction. Nothing else.

  • No confirmation step: you're on the list the moment you sign up
  • From the agency's own notice, with the link
  • Unsubscribe anytime

Why only official sources

Auction listing sites often copy each other, and dates go stale. We read the county or state agency's own page, note the date we checked it, and link you straight to it. The auction sites agencies use get a link so you can bid, but we never take dates or details from them. Before you bid, always read the agency's terms of sale. Tax-defaulted property is sold as-is, and the agency's rules and deadlines are what count.
